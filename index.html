Our Story
Four women.
One obsession.
Zero waitlists.

We are Katherine, Alexia, Jissela, and Mom — a family of women who share more than a love for fashion: we share a fascination for the details that make any moment feel special.

Over the years, we built our own collection of dream bags. And yes, we learned that getting one could take years. Too long for something that should simply make you happy.

Then we discovered mini bag charms. And we fell in love instantly. Because it is not about having a Birkin. It is about that feeling. Elevating any bag you already love.

We created I'm not a Birkin for every woman who loves luxury and that little thrill that transforms a whole look. No waitlists. No complications.

You don't need a Birkin to feel that thrill.
— Katherine, Alexia, Jissela & Mom ✨
