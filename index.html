Our Story
Four women.
One obsession.
Zero waitlists.

We are Katherine, Alexia, Jissela, and our mother — a family of four women who share one great love: Hermès. Our mother and Jissela are designers. Katherine and Alexia? Completely obsessed with bags.

Over the years, we patiently built our own Birkin collection, waiting up to two years on a waitlist for a single bag. If you know, you know.

When we discovered the mini Birkin charm, we fell in love instantly. The perfect detail to hang from any bag — not just a Birkin or a Kelly, but any bag you already own and love.

"You don't need a Birkin to wear one. And you definitely shouldn't have to wait two years to feel that kind of joy."
— Katherine, Alexia, Jissela & Mom ✦
